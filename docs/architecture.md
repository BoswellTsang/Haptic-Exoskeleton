# Power architecture — public research overview

The project involves battery-powered BLDC actuation and lower-voltage control electronics. General electrical design topics investigated include:

- An energy-storage subsystem with appropriate pack protection, voltage/current monitoring and cell management.
- A higher-voltage motor drive path and regulated 5 V / 3.3 V control electronics supply paths.
- Power-conversion efficiency, allowable input-voltage range, thermal limits and electrical noise.
- A protection strategy addressing reversed battery polarity, excessive current, transient voltage and motor disabling.

These are **general architectural concepts**, not a release of the team's actual internal schematic or an approved hardware design. No operational safety claim is made.
